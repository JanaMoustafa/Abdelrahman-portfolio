Majlis مجلس

A financial council for Egyptian founders and SMEs. Up to eight AI advisors debate a money decision before you make it. They work from your own documents, Egyptian law and calculations done by tested code.

Each advisor answers alone first, so nobody copies the loudest voice. Then they challenge each other, the Auditor deletes any claim without a source, and the Chair gives one verdict and names the dissent. Answers come in English or Egyptian Arabic, and the Teacher explains them at your level.

Business modelB2B for founders and SMEs. Monthly plans in Egyptian pounds, from a free tier to the full eight-advisor council, with a locked price for founding members.

DiaDose

My graduation project: an AI assistant for people with diabetes. Photograph a meal and it works out what's on the plate, the carbs, a dose, and the next 24 hours of glucose. The paper behind it is accepted in Informatica (Web of Science, IF 3.429).

Illustrative curve. The shaded band is the 70–180 mg/dL target range.

  1. Recognise the food

    A two-stage transfer-learning run on an EfficientNetV2-S backbone reads a photo, or a typed dish name, and ranks all 101 Food-101 classes.

    86.63% top-1 and 97.43% top-5 accuracy

  2. Estimate the nutrients

    A regression head trained on Google's Nutrition5K predicts carbohydrates, energy, fat and protein from the same image.

    Mean error of 5.18 g carbs and 60 kcal

  3. Recommend a dose

    A seven-layer dosing engine follows ADA 2024, ISPAD 2019–2024, WHO and NICE guidance: insulin-to-carb ratio, sensitivity factor, HbA1c calibration, dawn-phenomenon correction, glycaemic index and BMI tiers for Type 2. Drag the sliders to run the base formula yourself.

  4. Forecast the next 24 hours

    A BiLSTM, a CNN-LSTM with attention and a Transformer forecast glucose together as a weighted ensemble. SHAP values explain every forecast, so a patient can see why the curve bends.

    An R² of 0.9859

  5. Ship it

    A FastAPI and SQLite app with 18+ REST endpoints, JWT and bcrypt auth, a live patient dashboard for glucose, water and wellness, 7-day meal plans, risk alerts, and a nutrition chatbot running LLaMA 3.3 70B.
